# KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md

> The honest record of what this project could **not** do or achieve, and why. Filled by the executor (dsh / flash v4) the moment it hits a wall, and by the human whenever one is discovered.
>
> Two kinds of entries:
> 1. **Limitations** — something couldn't be achieved for a legitimate reason (model capability, platform limit, external constraint), with evidence.
> 2. **Deferred work** — features/functionality we consciously chose not to implement yet, tracked through `TODO[T<n>]` comments in the code.
>
> An undocumented limitation will resurface later disguised as a bug. Write it down instead.

## Rules

- The executor adds an entry **the moment** it hits the limitation — not at the end of the milestone, not "later".
- Faking success (silent catches, rigged tests, claiming a result without evidence) is a process failure. A limitation written here is normal engineering; a hidden one is not.
- TODOs without an index row are treated as out-of-scope work and are grounds for rejecting the milestone.
- When a limitation is resolved or a TODO is completed, don't delete the row — mark it `resolved in M<n>` / `done in M<n>` and say how.
- New entries from a milestone are listed in `STATUS.md` and committed with that milestone.
